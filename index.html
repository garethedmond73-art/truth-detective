The Truth Detective

An open-minded investigation into big questions.

How to use the Truth Detective
  1. Pick a question.
  2. Investigate and read the answer.
  3. Check the evidence in the ledger.
  4. Explore connections between ideas.
  5. Ask what could prove an idea wrong.

A clue is not proof. Lots of clues do not automatically make a theory true.

Five witness groups

Scripture

Bible, Guru Granth Sahib, Torah, Quran and other traditions.

Science

Physics, biology, neuroscience and cosmology.

Philosophy

Consciousness, identity, reality and existence.

Human experience

Personal accounts, dreams, history and lived experience.

Symbols & language patterns

Symbols, metaphors, number patterns, word roots, translation differences, repeated phrases, geometry and motifs across traditions.

Our rule: record what we observe separately from what we think it means, then test alternative explanations.
